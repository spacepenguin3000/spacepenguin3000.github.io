Wiring diagram reading

Trace source → circuit protection → switches/relays → connectors → load → return. Confirm connector IDs, pin numbers, wire numbers, grounds and effectivity in the WDM.

Fault isolation workflow

  1. Verify the reported symptom and aircraft configuration.
  2. Review applicable AMM / FIM / TSM and safety precautions.
  3. Identify source, protection, control and return paths.
  4. Measure with the specified method and loading conditions.
  5. Isolate the fault and perform approved corrective action.
  6. Complete required operational tests and records.

Measurement reminder

Seeing 28 V on an unloaded conductor does not prove the circuit can supply current. Under approved test conditions, compare voltage across the load and voltage drops in supply and return paths.
